# Contributing

Keep this repo practical.

Good contributions:

- Concrete production risks
- Reusable launch gates
- Agent prompts with clear output formats
- Lightweight checks with low false-positive rates
- Templates teams can actually fill out

Avoid:

- Generic startup advice
- Unsourced claims
- Vendor hype
- Heavy frameworks
- Compliance promises

When adding a checklist item, prefer this shape:

```text
- [ ] Specific condition that can be inspected or tested.
```

Also record:

- The earliest launch stage where absence becomes a required action
- The feature or evidence that makes the check applicable
- A primary source when the check depends on a standard, provider, protocol, or changing external requirement
- A fixture test when the scanner automates any part of the check

Keep the beginner path short. New specialized checks belong in focused reference checklists unless they prevent a common, high-impact failure for most apps at that stage.
